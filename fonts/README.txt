Zilla Slab
==========

Used for headings and figures in CageTrack, per the Peterman Brothers Brand
Guide (p.13: "Zilla Slab Bold — used for headlines on websites and other
digital properties").

These are the Latin-subset WOFF2 files from Google Fonts (Zilla Slab v12),
self-hosted so the dashboard doesn't depend on fonts.googleapis.com being
reachable. Weights included: 400, 500, 600, 700.

Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 — free to use, embed and redistribute,
including commercially. Full text: https://openfontlicense.org
Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Zilla+Slab

To update: download the latest WOFF2 files from Google Fonts and replace the
files here, keeping the same names. The @font-face rules are at the top of
styles.css.
